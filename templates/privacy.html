Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic usage information — such as login times, used to keep your account secure.

We do not ask for or store bank account numbers, card details or other payment credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and category breakdowns.
  • Protect the service against unauthorised access and abuse.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database used only to operate Spendly. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information and expenses are removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties, except where required by law. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of serving those files. Any future third-party services will be listed in this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to access or delete your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
